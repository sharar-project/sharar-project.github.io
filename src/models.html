Serving profiles

Every model runs under the same three profiles. Pick one with a flag on sharar serve; each knob can still be set on its own.

--exact

The model's own outputs: original weights and exact verification. Use it for benchmarks.

fast (default)

3-bit decode weights, expert sharing, NVFP4 KV on one Spark, and relaxed acceptance where it holds accuracy. Up to 4 requests decoded together.

--throughput

The fast profile with many more requests decoded together (16 on the 27B, up to 128 on Flash-Next) and a smaller recurrent state.
